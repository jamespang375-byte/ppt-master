# Huawei Color Scheme (华为配色) Brand Specification

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Brand Name | Huawei Color Scheme Style / 华为配色风格 |

## II. Color Scheme

| Role | HEX | Provenance | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary | `#C00000` | fact | Huawei Red — authoritative and dominant brand color |
| secondary | `#1F497D` | fact | Deep Steel Blue — classic business/engineering contrast color |
| accent (warm) | `#FBA000` | fact | Warm Gold / Yellow — used for milestones, certifications, and awards |
| accent (alert) | `#E9002F` | fact | Bright Red — minor alerts and highlight callouts |
| text | `#333333` | fact | Charcoal Gray — professional primary body text |
| muted-text | `#666666` | fact | Medium Gray — secondary descriptions and annotations |
| bg | `#FFFFFF` | fact | Clean White — standard presentation slide background |
| surface | `#F2F2F2` | approx | Light Gray — secondary card backing or zebra table rows |

The color scheme anchors around a solid red and deep blue motif. Red serves as the main highlight for titles, key statistics, and main indicators, while deep blue provides structure for table headers, accent boxes, and text frames.

## III. Typography

| Role | Family | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| title | `"Microsoft YaHei", "微软雅黑", "SimHei", Arial, sans-serif` | 700 |
| body | `"Microsoft YaHei", "微软雅黑", Arial, sans-serif` | 400 |
| data | `Georgia, Arial, sans-serif` | 600-700 |

Title typography is bold and solid to give an authoritative structure. Body text uses Microsoft YaHei for high readability across displays. Financial or mathematical data highlights can use Georgia for a classic, sophisticated numerical style.

## V. Voice & Tone

- Formality: formal / high-professional
- Person: we / 我们 (representing the organization)
- Emoji: avoid
- Abbreviations: spell-out-first / standard-abbrev-allowed
- Style: structured, report-oriented, emphasizing delivery, scale, technological leadership, and engineering excellence

## VI. Icon Style

- Preference: filled / solid stroke icons
- Recommended libraries: `tabler-filled` or `chunk-filled`
- Style notes: Icons should be sturdy and clear. Avoid overly decorative or abstract icon designs; prioritize logical and standard corporate symbols.
